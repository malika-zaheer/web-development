Small-batch scoops, big local roots

Sweet & Swirl started in a single storefront with one churner and a stubborn belief: ice cream should taste like the real fruit, real cream, and real cocoa that went into it. No syrups. No shortcuts.

Every flavor is hand-churned in-house in small batches, using milk from farms less than 50 miles away and produce picked at peak season. If a flavor doesn't taste incredible, it doesn't make the case.
