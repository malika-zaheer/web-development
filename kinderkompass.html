3 Years: imagination, words, and "I can do it."

Many children at this age start combining words into short sentences, enjoy pretend play, and love repeating routines. Here's what families often explore — gently, at your own pace.

What children often practice now

Longer sentences, simple counting, pretend play with roles, and short focused activities.

This is general guidance, not a diagnosis. If you have concerns, please speak with your pediatrician or Kita staff.
