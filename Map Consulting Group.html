About Map Consulting Group

Every organization has a path forward. We help you find it.

Map Consulting Group was founded on a simple observation: mission-driven organizations rarely fail from a lack of passion. They stall from a lack of clarity — competing priorities, under-resourced boards, and strategies that were never quite built for the terrain ahead.

We started this firm to give nonprofits and associations what larger, impersonal consultancies often can't: candid guidance, grounded frameworks, and a genuine partnership through the messy middle of change.

"We believe every mission-driven organization deserves a clear, confident path to its next chapter."

How We Work

A clear road, from where you are to where you're going

Our process is deliberately linear — because clarity comes from moving through stages, not skipping them.

Step 1

Discover

We listen first — to your board, staff, and stakeholders — to understand the real terrain: strengths, tensions, and opportunities.

Step 2

Define

Together, we define what success actually looks like — clear priorities and goals your whole organization can rally around.

Step 3

Design

We build the actual roadmap: the strategies, structures, and timelines that turn ambition into an executable plan.

Step 4

Deliver

We stay alongside you through implementation, adjusting course as needed until the plan becomes lasting practice.
